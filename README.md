# surveillanceCheck
This is a python program I wrote in 2 HOURS for a creative assignment, showcasing concepts of surveillance, data breaching, and emotional responses to fictional media.

## Note for playing on your computer
This project works best on a Mac, buut I tried making it somewhat flexible. To play the game, simply download the .py file into the computer downloads folder, go to your Terminal, and run the program with "python3 surveillanceTest.py" or whatever your terminal prefers. If that does not work, I have provided a video of how the game is supposed to work in the repo!

For my project I did not use any AI other than the occasional Google search, which unfortunately and inevitably provides the AI overview. The project I decided to do is an in-terminal game-like experience that reflects on Behaviorism topics such as surveillance and irrational panic response to media. The idea that a piece of fictional media can incite an emotional response from you due to a lack of knowledge on the topic being handled in the media is highly related to the concept of behaviorism and how people interact with different forms of media throughout history. Susceptibility to belief is something I played around with while making this, aiming to see how far I could go with making the piece believable while still keeping it somewhat narratively concise. On the other hand, I liked also playing with the idea that we download and open access to countless apps, websites, etc. that take our data and information every day. We've become heavily desensitized to the concept of privacy when it comes to our technology, and that can get scary very quickly the closer you look into it. I would have loved to incorporate some more narrative choices if I had more time, but this was what I had time for. To be clear, I did not steal any of your information! The script is completely local, so it did not actually save, share, or steal any of your data/files/info haha.
